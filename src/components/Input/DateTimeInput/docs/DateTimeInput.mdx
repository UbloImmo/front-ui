## Usage

`DateTimeInput` component combines a [DateInput](?path=/docs/components-forms-input-dateinput-usage--docs) and a [TimeInput](?path=/docs/components-forms-input-timeinput-usage--docs) side by side, a specific type of [Input](?path=/docs/components-forms-input-usage--docs).
It is commonly used in form layouts to prompt users for a precise moment in time, such as an appointment, a deadline or a check-in.

Each field keeps the behavior of its standalone counterpart: users may type into it, or use its popover (a calendar for the date, a time picker for the time).

<Canvas of={DateTimeInputStories.Default} />

### Value & Formatting

`DateTimeInput` is initialized without a value. It consumes and reports a single date-time **ISO string** (e.g. `2024-08-14T10:30:00+02:00`), which is split under the hood between its two fields.

> While either field is cleared or incomplete, `DateTimeInput` will report its value as being `null`.

<Callout>
  Providing a value that is not a valid date-time ISO string logs a warning, and the input is treated
  as empty.
</Callout>

<Canvas of={DateTimeInputStories.Values} />

### Setting a date & a time

The input is filled in two steps, as a time cannot exist without a date.

#### Date first

The time field is **disabled until a date is set**.

When a date gets picked for the first time, the time defaults to the time of the `min` property if that date is the `min` date, or `00:00` otherwise. From then on, picking another date **keeps the time that was already set**.

> Clearing the date clears the whole value, time included.

#### Then time

Once a date is set, the time field becomes available. Changing the time only updates the time of the current date, seconds and milliseconds are reset to `0`.

> The time field reports an empty or incomplete time as `null`, which `DateTimeInput` ignores: the previous time is kept until a valid one gets entered.

### Minute steps

Some use-cases only allow users to select minutes at a regular interval (e.g. appointments every 15 minutes).

`DateTimeInput` accepts an optional `step` property, expressed in minutes, that gets forwarded to its time field. Only minutes falling within the provided interval are available in the time picker.

> `step` is clamped between `1` and `60` minutes and defaults to `1`.

<Canvas of={DateTimeInputStories.Step} />

### Limiting selectable values

`DateTimeInput` accepts `min` and `max` properties, as date-time ISO strings, that dictate what dates and times are available for selection.

<Callout>Both `min` and `max` properties are **inclusive**.</Callout>

Each bound is split just like the value:

- its **date** limits what days can be picked in the calendar,
- its **time** limits what hours & minutes can be picked in the time picker.

The time bounds depend on the currently selected day:

- On the `min` date, times earlier than the `min` time are disabled.
- On the `max` date, times later than the `max` time are disabled.
- On any day strictly between the two, every time is available.
- If `min` and `max` share the same date, both time bounds apply.

> When picking another date, the current time is **clamped** to the bounds applying to that date. Picking the `min` date while the time is earlier than the `min` time moves it to the `min` time, and picking the `max` date while it is later than the `max` time moves it to the `max` time.

#### Minimum

Set the component's `min` property to disable selecting any earlier date or time.

Its time is also used as the default time when the `min` date itself gets picked for the first time.

<Canvas of={DateTimeInputStories.Min} />

#### Maximum

Set the component's `max` property to disable selecting any later date or time.

<Canvas of={DateTimeInputStories.Max} />

#### Within a range

Providing both `min` and `max` properties works as expected in most cases. Both limits are treated as **inclusive**.

Time bounds follow the same rules as [TimePicker](?path=/docs/components-forms-timepicker-usage--docs), including ranges wrapping around midnight (e.g. from `21:00` to `05:30`).

<Canvas of={DateTimeInputStories.MinMax} />

### Placeholders

Both fields can display their own placeholder while empty:

- `placeholder` applies to the date field, and to the time field when `timePlaceholder` is unset.
- `timePlaceholder` overrides the time field's placeholder.

### Disabled

Set the `disabled` property to `true` to prevent any interaction with both fields and their popovers.
